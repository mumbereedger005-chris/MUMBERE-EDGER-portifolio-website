My Story

Hello! I'm Mumbere Edger, an Information Technology student at Bugema University, Kampala Campus. My journey into technology began with a deep curiosity about how digital systems shape the world around us.

Growing up, I was always fascinated by computers and how software could solve real-world problems. That fascination drove me to pursue a degree in Information Technology, where I've been developing both my technical skills and my ability to think critically about design and problem solving.

At Bugema University I've worked on academic and personal projects that have strengthened my foundation in web development, database systems, and software design. I believe in continuous learning and actively seek opportunities to grow beyond the classroom — through online courses, community participation, and building real projects.

My goal is to become a skilled software developer who contributes to meaningful technological solutions — particularly those that address real challenges faced by communities in East Africa and beyond.
